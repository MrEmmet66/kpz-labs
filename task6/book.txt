The Great Journey

Chapter 1

In the beginning, there was darkness. The world was formless and empty, and shadows covered the deep waters.

 This is how our story begins, with nothing but potential waiting to unfold across the cosmos.

It was a time before time itself had meaning, when the universe was young and full of possibilities.

 Many scholars have debated the exact nature of these early moments, but none can truly know what came before.
